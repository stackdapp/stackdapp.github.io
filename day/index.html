Why it works

Structure that makes sense to their brain.

Most routine apps are just checklists. Stackd Day is a visual game with XP, levels, and rewards that make kids want to participate.

🗓

Visual schedule, not a list

Kids see their whole day in blocks: Wake Up, School, Free Time, Bedtime. No surprises. No "what's next?" meltdowns.

⚡

XP for everything

Every task and block earns XP. Kids level up through ranks and spend points on rewards you control.

⏱

Gentle transition warnings

Soft visual cues before a block ends. Transitions stop being a shock. The day flows.

🔒

Parent controls everything

Set a PIN and kids can use the app freely. They can't edit their own rewards or settings without you.

🎨

5 themes, their choice

Special Ops, Superhero, Enchanted, Sprout, or Modern. The app feels like theirs because it is.

📵

No ads, no data collected

Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads. Free to start.
